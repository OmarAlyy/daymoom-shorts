## Hook — three options

1. **"This time will be different."** The balloon drops in, bounces and glints. *(picked)*
2. "Every New Year's goal dies in 3 weeks." Rejected: a stats claim with no source.
3. "Your goal is too big." Rejected: blunt, and it gives the payoff away in the hook.

Why #1: everyone has said this line, so it lands as instant recognition. The irony ("Mm-hm. Sure.")
sets up a tension that only resolves at 24s ("1% a day"). Five words fit the 7-word cap, and the
gold "different." is the one key word.
