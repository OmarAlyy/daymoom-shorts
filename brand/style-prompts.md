# Balloon — owner's prompts (Nov 2026)

These prompts come from the owner, verbatim. They are written in English because image and video tools follow English more precisely.
Every request is assembled the same way: (1) the character prompt first, then (2) the style prompt, then (3) the idea template.
If a tool accepts a reference image, attach one of the states (e.g. `idle.png`).
Keep only ONE of the two colours in brackets.

## 1. Character (fixed)

```
CHARACTER: "Balloon" — a cute party balloon mascot, flat 2D vector style.
- Body: a smooth oval balloon, slightly taller than wide, one solid flat color [ORANGE #D9431E / TEAL #2FA59B], no outline, no gradients.
- A small curved cream highlight on the upper-left of the body.
- A tiny darker knot at the bottom and a thin dark wavy string hanging down like a tail. No legs.
- ALWAYS wears round black sunglasses with a small curved bridge; a thin cream glint line on each lens. The eyes are NEVER visible.
- Expressive thin black eyebrows above the sunglasses and a simple black line mouth below them.
- Two thin black line arms with small round fists (open 4-finger hands when waving).
- Personality: calm, confident, cool, quietly witty. Never loud, never sarcastic or mocking.
- Emotion is shown ONLY through eyebrows, mouth, arm gestures, and body squash/stretch.
Keep proportions, colors, and line weights identical in every frame.
```

## 2. Video style (fixed)

```
VIDEO STYLE:
- Vertical 9:16, 30–70 seconds, flat 2D animation, clean and minimal.
- Background: one warm solid color (cream #F4EFE6 or soft peach), simple flat props only when the story needs them, no clutter.
- Supporting characters: simple flat shapes in the same style, muted colors, so Balloon stays the focus.
- Motion: Balloon gently floats up and down, string sways; squash and stretch on reactions; eyebrows change first, then mouth 2–3 frames later.
- Signature move: the sunglasses glint after every punchline or key line.
- The black lenses can reflect what Balloon is looking at.
- Captions: word-by-word, bold white rounded sans-serif with a black outline, centered in the lower third; the key word highlighted in Balloon's color.
- Sources/years appear as small clean cards (e.g., "1930 · Keynes").
- Audio: calm, slightly low, confident narrator voice, like telling a secret; light background music; cute SFX (balloon squeak, air puff, soft pop).
- Structure: shocking hook in the first 2 seconds → short story → twist → one-line wisdom → soft ending.
```

Silent version — add to the style:

```
SILENT VERSION: no narration. Tell the story through Balloon's body language, on-screen counters (e.g., "Day 1", "Said yes: 4"), music that shifts with the mood, and ONE short text line at the end.
```

## 3. Idea template

```
STORY: [the idea or story in two lines]
HOOK (first 2s): [the opening shocking line or question]
TWIST: [the turn at the end]
WISDOM (one line): [the lesson]
FACT + SOURCE (optional): [a number or year and its source]
LENGTH: [30 / 45 / 60] seconds
VERSION: [with narrator / silent with music and SFX only]
```
